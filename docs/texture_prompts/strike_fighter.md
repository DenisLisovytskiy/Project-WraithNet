WraithNet frame `STRIKE_FIGHTER`: default workhorse (speed 5, 7 ordnance slots, 5 HP). Drawn today as a colored dart. This is the “normal jet” every default scenario uses.

## Prompt

```
Game-ready concept render of a fictional sixth-generation multirole strike fighter, single aircraft only, centered in frame.

Medium-size single-seat fighter-bomber with a blended fuselage, moderately swept wings, a single vertical tail, and two modest engine nozzles. Looks like a versatile strike jet: more wing area and a thicker belly than an interceptor, still clearly a fighter not a bomber. Closed weapon bays, no hanging stores.

Paint: medium cool gray with subtle panel lines and a faint teal sheen. Large clean dorsal and wing surfaces for later tinting. No flags, no tail codes, no manufacturer logos, no kill marks.

Camera: three-quarter view from slightly above and ahead, aircraft flying toward the upper-right, wings level. Orthographic / very long lens. Isolated on a fully transparent background.

Lighting: cool moonlight from upper left, thin cyan rim light, sharp silhouette, PBR military game asset, high detail but not photoreal clutter.

Square 1024x1024, PNG, transparent background, single subject filling about 80% of the frame.
```
