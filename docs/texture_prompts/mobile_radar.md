```
Heavy 8x8 military truck with a large rotating planar AESA / folding dish radar array raised on a mast above the cab. The radar face is the hero: a hexagonal or diamond-shaped panel, pale blue-gray, clearly a sensor not a weapon. Outriggers down, no missile tubes, no gun.

Lighting: cool moonlight, stronger cyan glints on the radar face, PBR game asset, sharp edges.

Square 1024x1024, PNG, transparent background, subject filling about 80% of the frame.
```
