# Texture prompt — AAA_LIGHT

WraithNet frame `AAA_LIGHT` (target type `AAA_SITE`). Short-range inaccurate guns, 2 HP, no missiles. Drawn today as an orange disc/glyph. Must look like a light gun pit, not a SAM TEL.

## Prompt

```
Game-ready concept render of a fictional light anti-aircraft artillery emplacement, single pit only, centered in frame.

Open circular gun pit: twin-barrel 35mm-class towed AA cannon on a small rotating mount, sandbags, ammo crates, a simple optical sight, no radar van, no missile tubes. Light, exposed, almost improvised compared to a turreted SPAAG.

Paint: dusty ochre and olive, gun metal dark iron, muzzle slightly sooty. Accent color family around RGB 230,140,40 (the current AAA map color). No flags, no crew, no text.

Camera: elevated three-quarter view, about 40 degrees down, circular pit obvious from above. Orthographic / long lens. Isolated on a fully transparent background.

Lighting: cool moonlight, warm orange glint on the barrels, PBR military game asset, sharp silhouette.

Square 1024x1024, PNG, transparent background, subject filling about 80% of the frame.
```

```
missile launcher, SAM, Patriot, tank, bunker, Gepard photo, Pantsir photo, people, soldiers, national flag, text, explosions, tracer fire, aircraft, cartoon, sky, watermark
```

- Open pit + twin barrels; no armored turret (that is `AAA_TURRETED`)
